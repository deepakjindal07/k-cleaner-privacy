03 / GUIDING PRINCIPLES

How we think about building software.

We believe good software should be dependable, transparent, and respectful of the user's attention.

01 / PRINCIPLE

Useful by design

We focus on solving genuine problems rather than adding unnecessary complexity. Every feature must serve a clear purpose, without gimmicks, bloated background processes, or misleading statistics.

02 / PRINCIPLE

Simple experiences

Software should be approachable from the first tap. We favor calm typography, uncluttered layouts, and transparent interactions that leave users feeling in control of their device and data.

03 / PRINCIPLE

Continuous improvement

Great utilities are refined through careful testing and listening to real feedback. We iteratively optimize performance, fix edge cases, and adapt to modern operating system standards.
