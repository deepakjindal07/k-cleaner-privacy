KAD Labs is an independent software studio founded and operated by Deepak Kumar (“we”, “us”, or “our”). We design and develop practical digital utilities and applications. Our website is located at https://kad-labs.com.

2. Scope of this Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you visit and interact with the KAD Labs website. We are committed to transparency and limiting data collection to what is strictly necessary to provide information about our products.

3. Information Collected on this Website

A. Information You Voluntarily Provide: If you send us an email or submit an inquiry, we receive the details you choose to share (such as your name, email address, and the contents of your message). We use this information solely to respond to your inquiry and provide product support.

B. Technical Server Logs: Like virtually all websites, our web hosting provider (such as GitHub Pages or cloud hosting infrastructure) may automatically process standard HTTP request logs (such as IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URL, and timestamps) to deliver web pages securely, monitor server reliability, and protect against malicious activity.

C. Tracking and Cookies: The KAD Labs static website does not set third-party tracking cookies, user profiling cookies, or invasive behavioral analytics scripts.

4. Third-Party Services & Links

Our website may contain links to external platforms, such as Google Play or social channels. When you click an external link, you leave our website and become subject to the privacy terms and data practices of that third party. We encourage you to review their respective privacy policies.

5. How We Use and Protect Information

Any correspondence sent to us is kept confidential and is never sold, leased, or traded to third parties. We apply reasonable technical safeguards to protect communication channels, though no internet transmission is ever 100% immune from security risks.

6. Data Retention & Your Rights

We retain email inquiries only for as long as needed to address your feedback or maintain customer assistance. Depending on your jurisdiction (such as GDPR, CCPA, or applicable Indian data protection laws), you may have the right to request access to or deletion of correspondence you have sent us. You may exercise these rights at any time by contacting us.

7. Children's Privacy

Our website is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under applicable local legal age). If you believe a minor has contacted us with personal information, please notify us and we will promptly remove it.

8. Changes to this Policy

We may update this policy periodically to reflect website updates or regulatory requirements. Any modifications will be posted here with a revised effective date.
